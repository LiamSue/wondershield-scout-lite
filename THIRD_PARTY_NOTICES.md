# Third-party and format references

Python and its standard library, the linked SQLite library, and operating-system libraries are supplied by the runtime environment and are not bundled in this distribution. Their respective licenses remain applicable to those components.

This distribution does not bundle or import openpyxl or LibreOffice. Historical Office interoperability research referenced openpyxl 3.1.5 and LibreOffice core revision `33cc1a2ae1057b0668594fc70c8025a0b1e7efe1`. The openpyxl MIT license is retained at `LICENSES/openpyxl-MIT.txt` for reference; its presence does not indicate that openpyxl software is included. The referenced LibreOffice source headers identify MPL-2.0 and incorporated Apache-work notices.

The present candidate does not include the earlier Office vendor implementations or their theme/palette resources. Whether any further attribution or license terms apply to any remaining expression requires review; this notice does not override the rights or terms of any third party.
